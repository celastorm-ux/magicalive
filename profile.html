Biography

Marcus Veil is a Los Angeles-based magician with over twelve years of professional experience. Specializing in psychological illusions and sleight of hand, Marcus has performed at venues ranging from intimate jazz bars to 2,000-seat theaters across the United States.

His style blends classic close-up magic with contemporary mentalism — creating shows that feel less like tricks and more like shared experiences. Off stage, Marcus teaches workshops on the psychology of perception and has been a member of the Society of American Magicians since 2015.
