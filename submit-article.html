Submission guidelines
✦All articles must relate to the world of magic — performance, history, technique, culture, or community
✦Minimum 300 words. Features and interviews work best at 600–2000 words
✦Content must be original and not published elsewhere
✦Our editorial team may lightly edit for clarity, grammar, and house style
✦Reviews should be of shows you actually attended — please note the date and venue

Tips for a strong submission
1Lead with a strong first sentence — something specific, surprising, or vivid
2Write for a reader who loves magic but isn't a professional — explain jargon
3For reviews, describe the experience as much as the tricks — atmosphere matters
4For technique pieces, include concrete examples and step-by-step clarity
5A cover image dramatically increases article engagement — include one if you can
