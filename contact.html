Common questions

Frequently asked questions

How do I create a magician profile? →
Click "Create profile" in the navigation, choose "Magician" as your account type, and follow the 6-step onboarding flow. It takes around 10 minutes to complete a full profile. Your profile goes live the moment you hit publish.
Is Magicalive free to use? →
Yes — creating a profile, posting shows, listing your venue, and reading articles are all completely free. We may introduce optional premium features in the future, but the core platform will always be free.
How do I get my venue listed? →
Email venues@magicalive.com with your venue name, address, capacity, and a brief description. We'll review it and add it to the directory within 5 business days. You'll then be able to claim and manage your venue page.
Can I submit an article if I'm not a magician? →
Absolutely. We welcome contributions from critics, historians, journalists, fans, and anyone with something interesting to say about the world of magic. Visit the Submit an Article page for guidelines.
How does the "online now" status work? →
When you're logged in and active on Magicalive, your profile shows a green "Online now" badge. When you're offline, it shows "Last active X hours ago." You can turn this off in your profile settings at any time.
Can I post events without a venue on Magicalive? →
Yes. When posting a show from your dashboard, you can type any venue name — it doesn't need to be an existing Magicalive venue. If the venue is listed, the show will also appear on the venue's profile automatically.
How do I report an issue with a profile or article? →
Use the contact form on this page with the topic "Bug report", or email support@magicalive.com. For content concerns such as inaccurate information or inappropriate content, please include the URL of the page in question.
How long does editorial review take? →
We review all article submissions within 3 business days and will email you either way. If your piece is accepted, we may reach out with light editorial notes before publishing. We read every submission we receive.
